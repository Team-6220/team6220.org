About

Making robots
Building leaders

Team 6220 is a student-led FIRST Robotics Competition team at Los Alamitos High School.

Team 6220 started in 2016 as a small group of Los Alamitos High School students working out of the Build-It Workspace. That first season we won the Orange County Regional and went to the World Championship.

After COVID we rebuilt from about a dozen students, and since then we have rewritten our design process, training, and team structure.

Student-led, student-driven

Our team is unique because of the immense focus we place on implementing our “student-led, student-driven” philosophy into everything we do. Our experienced mentors, mostly current or retired engineers, provide critical professional knowledge, safety experience, and legal responsibilities, but our students make nearly every final decision for our robot and the team.

As a result, our team enables students to gain vast amounts of hands-on experience not only in technical skills, but also in teamwork, leadership, and resourceful problem solving.

What is FRC?

The FIRST Robotics Competition is an international high school league. Each January a new game is revealed, and teams design and build a robot to play it in a few short months. Teams compete hard, then help each other in the pits: FIRST calls it Gracious Professionalism.

    Join the team

    Start with zero experience

    Most of our members had never touched a robot before joining. If you go to a Los Al USD school, there's a spot for you.

    1. Send us your interestFill out the form below so we can add you to Remind and our email list.
    2. Come to a meetingIn the fall we meet Tuesdays and Fridays 3:20–5:00 and Saturdays 10:00–2:00 in our workshop at Los Alamitos High.
    3. Pick a subteam and learnTry mechanical, electrical, programming, or business. Fall training gets you ready for build season in January.

    FAQ

    Do I need any experience?

    No. We train every new member in the fall, and students teach students. Curiosity matters more than knowing how to use a lathe.

    When and where do you meet?

    We meet in our workshop at Los Alamitos High School. In the fall: Tuesdays and Fridays 3:20–5:00 and Saturdays 10:00–2:00. During build season the shop is open every weekday after school until about 6:30–7:00, and Saturdays 10:00–4:00.

    I'm not into engineering. Is there a place for me?

    Yes. Our business, outreach, and media subteams run sponsorships, the website, social media, photography, and awards submissions.

    Can I join mid-year?

    Yes. Reach out and we'll get you caught up. Our summer camp is also a great way to start before freshman year.
